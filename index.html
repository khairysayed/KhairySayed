About Me

Welcome to my academic website. I am an Associate Professor in Electrical Engineering at Sohag University, Egypt. My work focuses on intelligent energy systems, renewable energy integration, power electronics, and electric mobility.

My professional experience combines university teaching, scientific research, and industrial experience in electricity distribution and supervisory control systems.

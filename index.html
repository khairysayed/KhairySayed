Academic Profile

Biography & Professional Background

Dr. Khairy Sayed is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Sohag University, Egypt. He received his B.Sc. in Electrical Power and Machines in 1997 from Assiut University, Assiut, Egypt. He pursued advanced graduate studies at the Electrical Energy Saving Research Center, Graduate School of Electrical Engineering at Kyungnam University, Masan, South Korea, earning his Master’s degree in 2007. In 2013, he earned his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Assiut University.

In 2024–2025, Dr. Sayed conducted advanced research in the United States as a prestigious Fulbright Research Scholar at the Smart Grid Interdependencies Laboratory, City College of New York (CCNY), City University of New York (CUNY). Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Ontario Institute of Technology in Canada (2016).

Dr. Sayed brings a uniquely valuable balance of industrial and academic expertise. Prior to his full-time academic appointment, he served for ten years (1998–2008) as a Power System Control Engineer at Middle Egypt Electricity Distribution Company, operating SCADA and Distribution Management Systems (DMS) for real-world municipal electricity grids.

Specialization & Innovation

Research Pillars & Interests

Pioneering solutions for clean energy integration, grid stability, intelligent conversion, and battery safety.

☀️

Renewable Energy Systems & Integration

Modeling, MPPT optimization, and grid integration of large-scale photovoltaic (PV) generation, wind turbines, and hybrid energy conditioners.

⚡

Smart Grids & Microgrids

AC/DC microgrid topologies, islanding protection, frequency and voltage stability, power quality mitigation, and resilient distribution automation.

🚗

Electric Vehicles & Energy Management

Vehicle-to-Grid (V2G) frameworks, smart EV charging schedule optimization, onboard DC-DC converters, and multi-vehicle fleet energy management.

🔌

Power Electronics & Conversion

Soft-switching high-efficiency DC-DC converters, resonant inverters, harmonic compensation, and high-frequency energy conversion topologies.

🧠

AI for Energy Systems

Artificial neural networks (ANN), fuzzy-neuro adaptive control, metaheuristics, and reinforcement learning for dynamic stability and fault classification.

🌐

Digital Twins & Intelligent Energy Hubs

Physics-informed digital twins for real-time state estimation, predictive transmission analytics, and co-optimization of multi-carrier smart energy hubs.

🔋

Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) & BMS

Advanced Battery Management Systems, short-term discharge State of Health (SoH) diagnostics, thermal safety protocols, and hybrid energy storage system (HESS) co-ordination for microgrid resilience.
